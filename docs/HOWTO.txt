NEO GEO MEDIA CENTRE: HOW TO USE THE FOUNDRY
=============================================

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- The Foundry: neogeo-media-centre-foundry.html, opened in a modern
  browser (Edge, Chrome or Firefox). Nothing to install. Nothing is
  uploaded: your music is converted inside the browser.
- Music: MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A or AAC, up to 64 tracks.
- To play it, one of:
    * a NeoSD (or similar) flash cart in a real Neo Geo, or
    * the MAME emulator with a MAME-format neogeo.zip BIOS.


STEP 1: ADD YOUR MUSIC
----------------------
1. Open the Foundry. Drag audio files onto the page, or click the feed
   slot to choose them.
2. Rename tracks in the ledger (names are upper-case, up to 32
   characters). Use the arrows to reorder and Remove to drop a track.
3. The ROM space gauge shows how much of the 16 MB cartridge you have
   used. "Auto-fit" picks the best sample rate that fits everything, so
   you can simply add music until the gauge is full. To choose by hand,
   turn it off and set a rate per track (8,000 to 55,500 Hz). Lower
   rates fit more music: halving the rate halves the space.
4. Leave "Even out loud and quiet tracks" ticked unless you want the
   original levels.
5. Press Preview to hear a track as the Neo Geo's YM2610 chip will
   play it. Only one track plays at a time.

Tip: you can save your work with "Save project" and carry on later with
"Load project".


STEP 2: CHOOSE A LOOK
---------------------
The "Cartridge look" panel restyles the Foundry and is baked into the
cartridge.

1. Click a skin. Twelve are on every cartridge, and the Neo Geo's
   options page has a SKIN row to switch between them. "Start on"
   chooses which one the cartridge begins with.
2. The small screen is a live copy of the Neo Geo screen. "Play demo"
   plays your list on it, "Try screensaver" shows the saver, "Show
   splash" plays the start-up splash.
3. Screen title: up to 20 characters at the top of the screen.
4. Animation: Calm, Normal or Lively.
5. Screensaver: Off, Dim, Partial dim, Bouncing title, Starfield, Warp
   starfield, Game of Life, Glyph rain, Light show, Gold lettering or
   Surprise me, plus how long the console waits before it starts. Any
   button wakes the screen.
6. "Screen filter" at the top of the page adds a CRT look to the
   Foundry itself. It does not change the cartridge.

Your choices are remembered in your browser.


STEP 3: BUILD
-------------
In the Export panel:

1. Set the Cartridge title and Maker if you like. A flash cart shows
   them in its game list.
2. Press "Build .neo" for a flash cart, or "Build MAME set" for MAME.

The Advanced section holds bare ADPCM-B audio files and a V-ROM image,
for people who want the raw data.


STEP 4A: ON A NEOSD FLASH CART
------------------------------
1. Copy the .neo file to your SD card, in its own folder.
2. Put the card in the NeoSD, switch on, and choose the game from the
   menu.

Work from a copy of the card's contents and keep a backup. Do not update
the flash cart's firmware on the same day as trying a new file, so that
if something goes wrong it is easy to trace.


STEP 4B: IN MAME
----------------
The package is laid out like a MAME folder:

1. Unzip UNZIP-INTO-MAME-FOLDER_<title>.zip INTO your MAME folder (the
   one with mame.exe) and let it merge. It places:
       roms\neogeo\ngmc.zip     the cartridge (leave it zipped)
       myhash\neogeo.xml        its software list entry
       run-ngmc.bat             double-click to start
2. Double-click run-ngmc.bat. It runs:
       mame neogeo ngmc -rompath roms -hashpath myhash
3. The Neo Geo BIOS (neogeo.zip, MAME format) must already be in
   roms\ in the MAME folder.

To try a new playlist, build again and unzip the new package over the
old one.


CONTROLS
--------
On a Neo Geo (stick or pad):

  Choose a track ........ Up / Down
  Previous / next ....... Left / Right
  Play highlighted ...... A
  Stop .................. B
  Repeat all on/off ..... C
  Shuffle on/off ........ D
  Menu / options ........ Start

In MAME (player 1 defaults; click the MAME window first):

  Up / Down / Left / Right ... arrow keys
  A ... Left Ctrl     B ... Left Alt     C ... Space     D ... Left Shift
  Start ... 1

Press Tab in MAME and choose Input (this Machine) to check or change a
key.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
* MAME lists "approximately matches" games
    It does not know ngmc. Check myhash\neogeo.xml exists, and start
    with run-ngmc.bat from the MAME folder.
* Checkerboard screen in MAME
    The zip was loaded as a raw cartridge. Use the command in step 4B.
* "sfix.sfix, sm1.sm1 NOT FOUND"
    Your BIOS zip uses FinalBurn names. Use a MAME-format neogeo.zip.
* A track is silent or the wrong speed
    Preview it in the Foundry first, then check its sample rate.
* Ctrl or Alt does nothing in MAME
    Click the window, or rebind the button in the Tab menu.
* The flash cart does not show the game
    Check the .neo file is in its own folder on the card, and try a
    fresh copy of the file.
